04 / PROOF OF CONCEPT
The POC is dynamic, not a static mockup.

Login & Security

Analyst authentication gate protects the console. Demo credentials (analyst@aegisnexus.com) allow controlled access to incident data.

Manual Recording

Analysts can record new incidents in real-time. The dashboard, risk engine, and attack graph update immediately from new data.

Evidence & Attachments

Supports URL, email (.eml), and voice recording attachments. Metadata and local content are stored within the incident record.

Persistence & Export

Local browser persistence keeps records across refreshes. Portable exports available in human-readable .txt and structured .json formats.

03 / THE WORKFLOW

The workflow is simple and auditable.

01

Record or Select

Start with a demo scenario or record a new incident manually in the console.

02

Attach Evidence

Paste URLs or upload email and voice files to support the investigation.

03

Review Surface

Inspect risk, confidence, graph relationships, and analyst notes in one view.

04

Approve Safely

Verify the recommended action and approve the simulated response.

05

Export Record

Download a portable text brief or JSON record for handoff and audit.

Every action is logged. Every decision is explainable.
